If you're getting ducted air conditioning installed, the controller is the part you'll actually touch every day. The **AirTouch 5** is the smart zone controller we install, and it's the one we recommend for most Perth homes.

Here's what it does, what it costs, and who it's worth it for.

## What is AirTouch 5?

AirTouch 5 is a **smart zone controller for ducted air conditioning**. It replaces the basic wall controller that comes with a ducted system, and adds a touchscreen wall console, an app, and individual control over each zone of your home.

It's designed to work with ducted systems from a wide range of brands (we confirm compatibility for your system when we quote), and AirTouch says it can manage multiple ducted or VRF systems in the same house from one place.

## What we like about it

### It's genuinely easy to use
The wall console is a clean touchscreen, and the layout is simple enough that the whole family can use it without a manual. That matters — a controller nobody understands ends up running every zone all day.

### Room-by-room temperatures
With AirTouch's temperature sensors, the system manages temperatures **throughout the home instead of in one spot**. Each zone can hold its own temperature, so the west-facing bedroom doesn't cook while the hallway near the controller feels fine.

### App and voice control
Switch zones on from your phone on the way home, check the house from work, or ask **Google Assistant or Amazon Alexa** to turn the bedrooms on.

### Room to grow into a smart home
It has **options for adding other smart home devices**, so it fits in if you're building out a smart home over time.

### Useful everyday features
According to AirTouch, the AirTouch 5 includes:
- **Quick Timer** and weekly **Programs** — bedrooms on at 9pm, living areas off overnight
- **Favourites** to save and recall your preferred setups
- **Sleep and Away modes** to save energy overnight or while you're out
- **Geofencing**, so the system can respond when you leave or head home
- **Temperature alerts** to your phone
- **Energy-use insights** so you can see what the system is using

## What it costs

On a **new ducted system with us, the AirTouch 5 upgrade is $1,000**.

The reason it's not more is that it **replaces the basic controller** that would otherwise come with the system — you're paying the difference to step up to smart zone control, not buying a second controller.

Adding AirTouch 5 to an **existing** ducted system is a different job (the existing controls and dampers need checking for compatibility), so we quote those individually.

## Things to know before you buy

- **Wi-Fi.** App control, voice assistants and alerts need the wall console connected to your home Wi-Fi.
- **Sensors matter.** To get true room-by-room temperature control, the zones need temperature sensors — we'll set this up as part of the install.
- **Zoning design matters more than the controller.** AirTouch 5 makes zoning easy, but the zones still need to be planned properly. See [how many zones you need](/blog/how-many-zones-ducted-air-conditioning).

## Who is AirTouch 5 worth it for?

- **Families** who want bedrooms and living areas at different temperatures
- Anyone who wants to **control the air con from their phone**
- **Smart-home** households using Google or Alexa
- Homes with **west-facing rooms** that run hotter than the rest of the house

If you only ever run one or two zones and are happy with a basic wall controller, you can skip it. But for most new ducted installs, the $1,000 is one of the best-value upgrades you can make.

## The bottom line

AirTouch 5 is a user-friendly smart controller that makes ducted zoning easy to live with. On a new ducted system from us it's a **$1,000 upgrade**, and for most homes it's worth it.
